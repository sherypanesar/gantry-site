Questions

Good to know

Do I need a computer at the machine?

No. With direct-connect you plug the controller into your phone over USB-OTG or pair over Bluetooth, and the phone is both the pendant and the G-code sender — nothing else on the bench. If you prefer, you can also run the free Gantry server on a shop PC and connect over Wi-Fi.

Which machines and firmware are supported?

Any controller on the standard GRBL path: GRBL 1.1, grblHAL and FluidNC. That covers Shapeoko, X-Carve, OpenBuilds LEAD/1010, MPCNC, LowRider and most home-built GRBL routers and lasers.

Is it really free?

Yes — core control, monitoring, the live 3D viewer, the built-in simulator and the alarm decoder cost nothing and need no account. Advanced features come as optional one-time in-app purchases. There is no subscription.

Can I try a paid pack before buying it?

Yes. Every pack's behaviour runs in the built-in simulator with no machine and no purchase, so you can see exactly what it does before deciding. Purchases are one-time and yours forever.

What data does Gantry collect?

None for the developer. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app only communicates with the controller or the server you connect it to. The privacy policy is public and you can inspect permissions on Google Play.

Is it safe to run a CNC from a phone?

A CNC machine is powerful and can cause injury regardless of what controls it. Gantry follows safe-machining practice — pre-flight checks, an always-visible state and emergency-stop — but you should always keep clear of the machine, know where your e-stop is, and never leave a running job unattended.
